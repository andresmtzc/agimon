What is Agimon?+

Agimon brings your customer conversations, your team and your AI teammate into one chat app—with the tools to get the work done right there.

Do my customers need to switch apps?+

No. Your customers keep using WhatsApp, Instagram, email and the other channels you connect. Your team handles those conversations together in Agimon.

What can my Agimon actually do?+

Answer questions, follow up with customers, book appointments, check stock, run campaigns and manage tasks. Your Agimon uses the conversations, tools and data in your workspace to help get the work done.

Can my Agimon work across conversations?+

Yes. Your Agimon can draw on the conversations, tools and data it has access to across your workspace. Ask which customers need a follow-up, find information across chats, or turn business activity into useful summaries and next steps.

Does my team stay in control?+

Yes. Your team and your Agimon share the same conversations and tools. You can step in, reply and do the work directly, while deciding what your Agimon can access and handle.

Can I use my own AI tools?+

Yes. Work with your Agimon, connect your own AI tools, or use both. MCP lets compatible AI tools access your workspace’s conversations, tools and data. You can also bring your own key to use Codex inside your Agimon group chats.

Is Agimon for my kind of business?+

Yes. Agimon works for businesses of every kind. Bring your customer conversations, team, tools and data together, and shape the workspace around the way you work.

How much does Agimon cost?+

Plans start at US$39 per month. Let’s talk about your team and the setup you need.
