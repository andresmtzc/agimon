Jitter landing-page reference copy
Source: https://jitter.video/

Purpose: Private visual baseline before any Agimon personalization.

Original page markup, styles, fonts, artwork, hero animation, header transitions,
dropdowns and video sources are preserved. Jitter and the featured creators
retain their respective names, artwork and other original materials.

Local adaptations are limited to reference-runtime.js, the noindex/CSP metadata,
disabling optional telemetry/billing bootstraps in five compiled modules,
and disabling route prefetch in the shared Link/NavLink wrappers. This reference
hosts the landing page only; hovering or focusing navigation must not load
the original application’s Templates/Pricing route modules.
Navigation and signup/login links open the original Jitter site. The newsletter
control opens the original site; this copy does not collect form submissions.
MP4 previews are still served by https://assets.jitter.video/.
